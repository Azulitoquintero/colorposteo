Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026.

ColorPosteo accesses TikTok only through TikTok's official APIs, after the account owner authorizes it via TikTok Login Kit. It uses the authorization solely to publish videos the owner chooses to post.

We store only the OAuth access tokens needed to operate, kept private and never sold, shared or used for advertising. We do not collect personal data from any other users. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's app settings, and stored tokens deleted on request.
